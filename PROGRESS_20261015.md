## Entry points and limits

`Inspect-Advisor-Mcp.ps1 -Country GER` or `-Country ITA` creates a protected fresh
campaign, installs the pinned modules, verifies a read-only advisor snapshot and
keeps the successful campaign paused/disarmed while its `native/hold-open.flag`
exists and AC remains connected. This launcher is for ongoing development.
Removing that owned hold flag triggers guarded closure/profile restoration.

`Inspect-OwnedLoad-Mcp.ps1` validates a previously verified owned save and keeps a
fresh loaded session open. It is currently Germany-specific; its readback driver
expects the verified deployment/save fixture. Do not use it as a general Italy
save loader.

`native-research-focus/native_mcp_server.py --session-file SESSION_FILE` exposes
the semantic tools. Inspect `verify_*_execution.py` and `inspect_*` for independent
verification of individual supported operations. Scripts are not a full AI policy.

The exact pinned game executable is required. Changing its accepted hash alone
cannot port offsets, virtual tables, native ABI or eligibility rules. Retain any
uncertain request and reconcile it before further actions. No game executable,
installed game content, saves, decompiler output, credentials, personal connection
scripts or private session evidence is published.
